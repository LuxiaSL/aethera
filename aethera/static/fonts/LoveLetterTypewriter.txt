Love Letter Typewriter
© 1994 Blake Haber, for Dixie's Delights. Distributed as a free font.

LoveLetterTypewriter.woff2 is the original LoveLetter.TTF (Fontographer
4.0.4, 1994-10-09) repackaged as WOFF2, glyphs unchanged. Its hdmx and kern
tables were dropped in 2026-11 (Firefox's sanitizer rejected both on every
load: hdmx without the head flags it needs, and kern holding only vertical
data that no browser applies), so rendering is unchanged. It has 106
glyphs (ASCII and a few more): no æ, no …, no arrows; browsers fall back
to the next font in the stack for those.

Used by oikos (the room at /) for the things in the room that are written by
hand or typed on paper: the monitors' name plates, the tape labels, and
the post titles on the transmissions screen.
